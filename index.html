Orrery

The solar system on any date, computed from JPL's orbital elements. Tilt it into three dimensions, follow a planet through its retrograde loop, or switch to the sky guide to see what is up tonight, with moon phases, star charts and eclipses to 2035.

martyn-gg.github.io/orrery

Field Guide to Figure Skating

A reference to figure skating elements and the national tests they appear in. Edges, turns, spins and jumps are drawn as diagrams of where the blade and the body go.
